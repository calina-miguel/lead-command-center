Lead Command Center Video Presentation Script

1. Command Center
Meet Lead Command Center, a client-ready growth workspace built for agencies that need faster follow-up, cleaner pipeline visibility, and fewer missed opportunities.

2. Priority View
The first screen shows what matters now: imported leads, positive replies, booked calls, and follow-up coverage, so the team always knows where to act first.

3. Hot Leads
Leads can move from BoldTrail intake into organized segments, then into outreach, reply sorting, call scheduling, proposal tracking, billing, and onboarding.

4. Content Studio
For outreach, the content studio creates review-ready email, social copy, image direction, and call-to-action links with consent checks and truthful claim controls built in.

5. Deployment Composer
Once content is approved, the user chooses the format, such as a post, reel, story, or My Day, selects the social platforms, attaches images or video, adds emoji tone, and queues the final deployment.

6. Apartment Intercom Context
For apartment communities, the story can transition into access and intercom workflows, showing how resident communication, visitor entry, and follow-up can sit beside the sales operation.

7. BEC Risk Awareness
The system also frames communication with security awareness, especially around business email compromise risk, where payment changes and urgent requests should be verified before action.

8. Pipeline
The pipeline view connects outreach to revenue, showing which prospects are new, which calls are booked, which proposals are active, and which clients are ready for billing.

9. Operating Engine
Under the hood, the workflow keeps the system consistent: intake, reply routing, content review, appointment preparation, closed-won handoff, and billing setup.

10. Client Value
For clients, the value is simple: faster answers, smarter follow-up, compliant outreach, and a system that turns attention into booked conversations.
